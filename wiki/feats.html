Feats

At level 4, and at other levels the GM allows, you may take a feat instead of an Ability Score Improvement. Feats are listed here. Each one has its prerequisite in brackets. Use the 5e rule for taking a feat (you may choose a feat only if you meet the prerequisite).

Street Wise

[None] You know how the streets talk.

  • Increase your WIS or CHA by 1 (maximum 20).
  • You gain proficiency in Insight or Persuasion (your choice).
  • Once per cycle, you can learn one rumor about any level of Vantara by spending a day asking around.

Quick Hands

[DEX 13] Your fingers are faster than your opponent's eyes.

  • Increase your DEX by 1 (maximum 20).
  • You may take the Use an Object action as a bonus action.
  • You have advantage on Sleight of Hand checks to pick a pocket or plant an item.

Fast Lane

[DEX 13] You move like you have somewhere to be.

  • Your speed increases by 5 feet.
  • You may Dash as a bonus action.

Tight Grip

[STR 13] Once you grab someone, they stay grabbed.

  • Increase your STR by 1 (maximum 20).
  • Your grapple DC is 8 + STR modifier + proficiency bonus.
  • When a creature tries to escape your grapple, it has disadvantage on the check.

Dead Eye

[DEX 13] At range, you do not miss on instinct.

  • Increase your DEX by 1 (maximum 20).
  • Ignore half cover when making ranged attacks.
  • Once per short rest, when you hit with a ranged weapon, you may add your proficiency bonus to the damage.

Brawler's Ledger

[STR 13] Every object in the room is a weapon.

  • You gain proficiency with improvised weapons.
  • Improvised weapons you use deal 1d6 damage.
  • When you hit with an improvised weapon, you may knock the target prone.

Reinforced Frame

[CON 13, Augmented] Your body was built to take what the city throws at it.

  • Increase your CON by 1 (maximum 20).
  • Your Load limit increases by 2.
  • Gain 1 extra hit point per level.

Efficient Surge

[Surge ability 13] You learned to stretch every spark.

  • Your maximum Surge increases by 1.
  • When you finish a short rest, you regain 1 Surge.

Steady Nerves

[WIS 13] Nothing you see can shake you.

  • Increase your WIS by 1 (maximum 20).
  • Advantage on saving throws against being frightened, charmed, or stunned.
  • Effects that would disable one of your augments (such as Lockout) have disadvantage against you.

Scrapper

[INT 13 or Techie role] You can fix nearly anything with a bad enough day and a good enough tool.

  • Increase your INT by 1 (maximum 20).
  • You gain proficiency with Techkit.
  • Repairing an item or augment takes half the normal time.

Mobile Rig

[INT 13 or Netrunner role] Your rig reaches farther than your hands.

  • Increase your INT by 1 (maximum 20).
  • Intrusions with a range of 60 feet or more gain 60 additional feet of range.
  • Once per long rest, you may cast one Intrusion without spending Surge.

Hard Target

[CON 13] You are difficult to pin down.

  • Increase your CON or DEX by 1 (maximum 20).
  • The first attack that hits you each round deals 1 less damage per level.
  • You have advantage on Constitution saves to maintain concentration on an Intrusion or effect.

Contact Network

[CHA 13] You are always someone's friend.

  • Increase your CHA by 1 (maximum 20).
  • You gain one Contact (see the Fixer role). You may use the Contact feature once per cycle even if you are not a Fixer.
