Glossary

Apex. The counterweight station at the top of the Tether. The highest level of Vantara.

Augment. A cybernetic part installed in a body. Costs Load. See Augments.

Black Market. Unlicensed augments and services, sold in SubZ. Cheaper, riskier, and sometimes traced.

Chrome ID. Elite clearance. Opens the Spires and Apex.

Civic ID. The basic identity every resident holds. Opens public services on every level.

Credits (cr). The currency of Corvanis.

Cycle. A span of downtime between sessions. Time passes in cycles for debt, rent, jobs, and news.

Debt standing. A character's status with their lenders: Clear, Overdue, Flagged, or Repossession Notice.

Deck. The rig a Netrunner uses to run Intrusions. Requires a Port Jack augment.

Favor. A Fixer's leverage die, spent to add a bonus to a roll.

Flux. A Helix stabilizer. Keeps augments running and prevents some Glitch effects.

Glitch. A malfunction that appears when an augment is overloaded or illegal. The GM rolls it on the Glitch table.

Grid, the. The middle level of Vantara. Factories, offices, transit, and worker housing. Needs a Shift Pass.

Intrusion. A Netrunner program that hits, disables, hides, or protects. Costs Surge.

Level (city). One of the four vertical levels of Vantara: Apex, the Spires, the Grid, and SubZ.

Lift 7. The freight and worker lift from SubZ to the Grid.

Load. The measure of how much augmentation a body can carry. Replaces Humanity. Your Load limit equals your Constitution score.

Load limit. The most Load a character can carry without a penalty.

No-data zone. A part of the city missing from the Registrar's records. Sector 0-9 and Sector 0-13 are the known ones.

Null Row. A SubZ street where notices are posted and gossip is traded.

Origin. Where a character was made, such as SubZ, the Grid, or a Helix lab. See Origins.

Overdrive. A way to push past zero Surge, at the cost of force damage and speed. See Surge.

Port Jack. A neural augment that lets a character jack into a deck.

Registrar. The public records office of Vantara. Keeps every resident's file.

Registrar hold. A block on a district or a person, placed by the Registrar.

Relic. A unique object with a save DC and charges. See Relics.

Repossession Notice. The final stage of debt standing. The lender has issued a claim on the character's collateral or body. The GM decides what this means.

Role. A character's class, reskinned for Corvanis. See Roles.

Shift Pass. Access to the Grid, issued at the Lift 7 desk.

Surge. The spark that runs through augmented bodies and rigs. Replaces spell slots. See Surge.

Tether, the. The single cable from the surface to Apex. The only lift to orbit.

Vantara. The largest city on Corvanis.

Vitalis Rescue. The city's private emergency medicine company, with coverage tiers by level.
