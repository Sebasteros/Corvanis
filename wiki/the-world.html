The World

Corvanis is a planet of towers, trade, and debt. Its largest city, Vantara, is built upward in levels around the Tether, the only lift to orbit. Every level tells you what you are worth.

A short history

The Tether was finished before most of the city above SubZ was built. Its counterweight station, Apex, went up first, and the people who paid for it moved in first. The city grew around the cable: factories and transit in the middle, towers above the smog line, and a ground level that was never meant to last.

Helix, the biotech conglomerate of the west, sold the first cybernetic limbs to workers who had lost their own. The Registrar followed, a public office that keeps the civil records of everyone who lives in Vantara. Within a generation, debt became the way most people got a limb, a home, or a job. Milia Trade Company bought the loans, and the Registrar kept the files.

Nobody planned the levels, but everyone uses them. Today the city runs on passes, IDs, and holds.

Vantara

Vantara is a free city. It is officially independent, which means its levels answer to the Registrar and to whoever holds the contract for a given district.

The four levels

Level Code Name Who lives there Access
Apex Orbit Apex The owners. Residents of the counterweight station. Chrome ID required
L2 L2 The Spires Executives, lawyers, and their households. Boarding for the Tether is at the Tether Gate. Chrome ID required
L1 L1 The Grid Factory workers, office staff, transit crews, and families. Shift Pass required
L0 L0 SubZ Everyone without a pass. Dense housing, night markets, and unlicensed clinics. Open (Registrar holds apply)

Apex: The counterweight station at the upper end of the Tether. Residential. Full coverage from Vitalis Rescue, and the only place a Tether ride ends.

The Spires: Executive and residential towers above the smog line. Clean air, quiet streets, and gates that check your ID at every corner.

The Grid: Factories, offices, transit, and worker housing. Public concourses only, and a constant hum of machinery. Lift 7 is the main freight and worker route up from SubZ, and the Shift Pass desk is there.

SubZ: The bottom of the city. Night markets, dense rooms, and clinics with no license. Most of the city's real work happens here, and so does most of its trouble.

Passes and IDs

  • Civic ID: Everyone has one. It opens public services and public areas on every level.
  • Shift Pass: Issued at the Lift 7 desk to people with a work permit. It opens the Grid.
  • Chrome ID: Elite clearance. It opens the Spires and Apex. It is rare, and it is watched.
  • Registrar hold: A block placed on a district or a person. A held character can enter SubZ but cannot use public services, and a hold on the Grid means a lost shift pass.

Null Row

Null Row is a street in SubZ where the city's notices are posted and its gossip is traded. It is the first place most characters will see, and it is the home of several key locations:

  • Terminal 0-114: A public Registrar kiosk. Maps, job postings, and account notices. Anyone can use it, and everyone can see who did.
  • Unit 4-C: A single room above a noodle stall, with two cots and a window that will not close.
  • The Gutterlight: The only bar on the block where nobody asks what you are carrying. The floor is sticky, and the jukebox skips.
  • Nine Bowls: A noodle house that stays open all night. Ask for Tavi if you are looking for work.
  • The Stitchery: A back-room clinic. Chrome repair, cash only, not a Helix-certified provider.

Other places

  • Lift 7 Checkpoint: The freight and worker lift to the Grid. A Shift Pass or work permit is required.
  • East Transit Gate: The road and rail exit from Vantara toward Kyrex and Vexmar. Subject to Registrar checks.

No-data zones

Some parts of the city do not appear in the Registrar's records. These are called no-data zones. Sector 0-9 lies in the north-east, above the Grid's old freight yards. Sector 0-13 lies in the south-west, below the older SubZ blocks. Nobody has a clean file for these places, and the GM decides what is inside them.

The Tether

The Tether is a single cable from the surface to the counterweight station, Apex. It is the only lift to orbit, and it is guarded and exclusive. Access to the Tether is a privilege, not a holy site, and it is controlled by the same companies that control the Spires.

Players may travel the Tether only with a Chrome ID or a sponsor's letter. A ride costs 500 cr one way (see Gear and Services).

Calendar and cycles

Time in Corvanis is counted in cycles. A cycle is a span of downtime between sessions, long enough for a job to finish, for news to change, or for a debt to grow. The GM decides how many days a cycle lasts in the story. At the start of each cycle, the GM works through the cycle steps.

The news

Corvanis has a public newswire. It runs headlines about Helix, the Registrar, the Tether, and the weather in SubZ. Read the news for clues. Some stories are true, some are not, and some are written to be read the wrong way.

Each level of Vantara is split into districts. Every district has its own job market, its own clinics, and its own rules about who can enter.
