Augments and Skill Trees

Augments are the cybernetic parts that make Corvanis characters more than human: limbs, eyes, skin, bone, and rig ports. In place of a Humanity score, Corvanis uses Load. Every augment costs Load. Your body can only carry so much before it starts to fail.

Load

  • Load limit: Equal to your Constitution score (plus any bonuses from your Origin or feats).
  • Load cost: Each augment has a Load cost, shown on its node.
  • Total Load: The sum of Load costs of all augments you own. It cannot be higher than your Load limit.
  • Over the limit: The GM may allow you to go over your limit (for example, by installing a Black Market augment). For each point over the limit, the GM rolls on the Glitch table once per long rest.
  • Lowering Load: Some augments and services can remove augments. Removing an augment refunds 1 Load per tier and costs half its price.

Tiers and prices

Tier Name Load cost Credit cost Where to install
I Common 1 300 cr Any clinic, or a Medtech in the field
II Mil-spec 2 900 cr Vitalis or Helix clinic, or a Techie with the Augment Smith specialty
III Prime 3 2,500 cr Vitalis or Helix clinic only
BM Black Market varies half the listed price The Stitchery, or any unlicensed clinic (see below)

Installation takes one cycle at a clinic. A Medtech with Cutting Edge can install a Common augment in a scene.

How the skill tree works

Augments are organized into five lines. Each line has three tiers, and each tier has two nodes. Owning a node gives you its effect.

  • Tier I: Any node in a line can be taken.
  • Tier II: You must own one Tier I node in the same line.
  • Tier III: You must own one Tier II node in the same line.
  • Starting node: Your Role gives you one Tier I node for free (listed in your Role).
  • Growth: The GM awards skill points. One skill point installs one node you qualify for, and you still pay its Load. A good pace is one point at each level beyond level 2, plus a point for a major story beat. You can also buy a node at a clinic for its credit price instead of spending a point.
  • Cross-line: You may take a node from a second line only if you own a Tier I node there.
  • Surge costs: Some node effects cost Surge. The Surge pool is covered in Surge.

Black Market augments

Black Market augments are cheaper and stronger, and they come with a cost. Each Black Market augment shows its Load and a Glitch Trigger: each time the trigger happens, roll on the Glitch table once.

Augment Load Price Effect Glitch Trigger
Rush Pump 2 450 cr For 1 minute, speed +10 feet and Dash as a bonus action. Costs 1 Surge. Each use
Blood Gate 1 150 cr As an action, heal 1d6 hit points, once per short rest. Each use
Hush Spool 2 450 cr For 1 minute, you cannot be seen by cameras or drones. Costs 1 Surge. Each activation

Synthetic chassis

Synthetics (see Origins) may take any augment line, but each augment costs 1 less Load (to a minimum of 1). Synthetics cannot use Vitalis Cover and cannot install Black Market augments without a Medtech.

Cyberware catalog

These augments sit outside the skill tree. Each one belongs to a line (shown in brackets) and can be installed at any clinic for its listed price.
