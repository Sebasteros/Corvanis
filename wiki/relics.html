Relics

Relics are unique objects with their own history. Some were made by a corporation and lost. Some were built in a SubZ back room and got too good. A Relic is a tool with a personality, a save DC, and a limited number of charges.

How relics work

  • Attunement: As in 5e, a character may attune to up to three relics at once. Attuning takes a short rest with the relic in hand.
  • Relic Save DC: Each relic has a save DC shown in its entry. It is set by rank (Standard 12, Rare 14, Unique 16). Use this DC for any save the relic forces.
  • Charges: A relic's charges come back on a long rest unless the entry says otherwise.
  • Finding relics: Relics are rare. The GM decides when a character finds one, and every relic has a reason it is in the city.

Standard relics

Ashline Knife

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. Attunement required.

A thin blade with a heat core. The edge is always warm.

  • Weapon: Simple melee weapon. 1d8 slashing damage, finesse.
  • Cauterize: Once per long rest, when you hit with the Ashline Knife, the target takes 2d6 fire damage and must make a CON save. On a failure, it bleeds for 1d4 damage at the start of each of its turns until it uses an action to stop the bleeding.

Quiet Hand

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. Attunement required.

A thin glove that fits over a bare hand or a chrome hand.

  • Silent Touch: You have advantage on Sleight of Hand checks made with this hand.
  • Lock Sense: Three times per long rest, you can open a mechanical or electronic lock by touch. The lock is not destroyed.

Rare relics

Hollow Rifle

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A rig rifle with a long, scratched stock. The barrel hums when it is loaded.

  • Weapon: Martial ranged weapon. 2d8 piercing damage, heavy, two-handed, range 150/600 feet.
  • Hollow Round: Three times per long rest, a shot ignores half cover. On a critical hit, the target's augments are disabled until the end of its next turn, as if by the Lockout intrusion.

Vesper Lens

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

An optic relic in a small brass frame. It was built for people to see what the city hides.

  • Read the Tag: Twice per long rest, as an action, you can see the ID marks, tags, and file notes of one creature you can see clearly. The GM describes what you find.
  • Lens Vision: You can see through walls up to 10 feet thick for 1 minute, once per short rest.

Sable Mantle

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A long coat with a mesh lining that eats light and sound.

  • Quiet Coat: You have advantage on Stealth checks while you are wearing the coat.
  • Mesh Armor: Once per short rest, when you take damage, you may gain resistance to that damage type for 1 minute.
  • Blind Spot: Drones and cameras cannot track you unless you are within 30 feet of them.

Mercy Syringe

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required (Medtech only).

A sealed injector with three doses of a restorative compound.

  • Dose: Three times per long rest, as an action, you may inject a creature you touch to restore 2d8 + WIS modifier hit points. The creature also ends one poison effect on itself.
  • Overdose: If you use the syringe on a creature that has already been dosed this cycle, the GM may roll on the Glitch table for that creature.

Unique relics

Gold Ledger

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A ledger bound in gold-colored cloth. Every entry in it is in ink that never fades.

  • Write the Debt: Once per cycle, you can write a debt into the ledger and transfer it to a willing creature. The transfer is recorded only in the ledger. The Registrar file does not change until someone checks.
  • Read the Ledger: Once per long rest, you can ask the GM one question about a debt, a contract, or a lender. The GM answers truthfully, though the answer may be partial.

Tether Anchor

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A clamp of dark metal with a counterweight on a cable. It was taken from the Tether's lower spool, and it still holds.

  • Anchor: As an action, attach the anchor to a surface within 60 feet. For 1 minute, the anchor can hold up to 2,000 pounds, or lift and lower a creature or object within 60 feet of it. Once per short rest.
  • Counterweight: You may spend an action to use the anchor as a shield against one melee attack. Your AC gains +2 against that attack.

Relics and the story

Every relic has a story that the GM can use. Ask the GM for it at the table. A relic that players do not understand is one the city will try to take.
