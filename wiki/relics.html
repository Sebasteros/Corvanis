Relics

Relics are unique objects with their own history. Some were made by a corporation and lost. Some were built in a SubZ back room and got too good. A Relic is a tool with a personality, a save DC, and a limited number of charges.

How relics work

  • Attunement: As in 5e, a character may attune to up to three relics at once. Attuning takes a short rest with the relic in hand.
  • Relic Save DC: Each relic has a save DC shown in its entry. It is set by rank (Standard 12, Rare 14, Unique 16, Legend 18). Use this DC for any save the relic forces.
  • Awakening: A relic can grow with the person who carries it. When its owner finishes a story that matters to the relic, the DM may awaken it: its save DC rises by 1 and it gains one more charge or use. A relic awakens once.
  • Charges: A relic's charges come back on a long rest unless the entry says otherwise.
  • Finding relics: Relics are rare. The DM decides when a character finds one, and every relic has a reason it is in the city.

Standard relics

Ashline Knife

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. Attunement required.

A thin blade with a heat core. The edge is always warm.

  • Weapon: Simple melee weapon. 1d8 slashing damage, finesse.
  • Cauterize: Once per long rest, when you hit with the Ashline Knife, the target takes 2d6 fire damage and must make a CON save. On a failure, it bleeds for 1d4 damage at the start of each of its turns until it uses an action to stop the bleeding.

Quiet Hand

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. Attunement required.

A thin glove that fits over a bare hand or a chrome hand.

  • Silent Touch: You have advantage on Sleight of Hand checks made with this hand.
  • Lock Sense: Three times per long rest, you can open a mechanical or electronic lock by touch. The lock is not destroyed.

Rare relics

Hollow Rifle

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A rig rifle with a long, scratched stock. The barrel hums when it is loaded.

  • Weapon: Martial ranged weapon. 2d8 piercing damage, heavy, two-handed, range 150/600 feet.
  • Hollow Round: Three times per long rest, a shot ignores half cover. On a critical hit, the target's augments are disabled until the end of its next turn, as if by the Lockout intrusion.

Vesper Lens

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

An optic relic in a small brass frame. It was built for people to see what the city hides.

  • Read the Tag: Twice per long rest, as an action, you can see the ID marks, tags, and file notes of one creature you can see clearly. The DM describes what you find.
  • Lens Vision: You can see through walls up to 10 feet thick for 1 minute, once per short rest.

Sable Mantle

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A long coat with a mesh lining that eats light and sound.

  • Quiet Coat: You have advantage on Stealth checks while you are wearing the coat.
  • Mesh Armor: Once per short rest, when you take damage, you may gain resistance to that damage type for 1 minute.
  • Blind Spot: Drones and cameras cannot track you unless you are within 30 feet of them.

Mercy Syringe

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required (Medtech only).

A sealed injector with three doses of a restorative compound.

  • Dose: Three times per long rest, as an action, you may inject a creature you touch to restore 2d8 + WIS modifier hit points. The creature also ends one poison effect on itself.
  • Overdose: If you use the syringe on a creature that has already been dosed this cycle, the DM may roll on the Glitch table for that creature.

Unique relics

Gold Ledger

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A ledger bound in gold-colored cloth. Every entry in it is in ink that never fades.

  • Write the Debt: Once per cycle, you can write a debt into the ledger and transfer it to a willing creature. The transfer is recorded only in the ledger. The Registrar file does not change until someone checks.
  • Read the Ledger: Once per long rest, you can ask the DM one question about a debt, a contract, or a lender. The DM answers truthfully, though the answer may be partial.

Tether Anchor

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A clamp of dark metal with a counterweight on a cable. It was taken from the Tether's lower spool, and it still holds.

  • Anchor: As an action, attach the anchor to a surface within 60 feet. For 1 minute, the anchor can hold up to 2,000 pounds, or lift and lower a creature or object within 60 feet of it. Once per short rest.
  • Counterweight: You may spend an action to use the anchor as a shield against one melee attack. Your AC gains +2 against that attack.

More standard relics

Null Coin

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. Attunement required.

A blank coin from a mint that no longer exists. It lands on its edge more often than it should.

  • Call It: Once per long rest, after you see the result of an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw you made, flip the coin and reroll. You must use the new roll.
  • Edge: The first time each cycle that you would roll on the Glitch table, the coin lands on its edge and you do not roll.

Dead Man's Deck

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. Attunement required (must own a Port Jack).

A scratched deck with someone else's initials on the case. It still has their shortcuts.

  • Old Paths: The save DC of your Intrusions increases by 1.
  • Stored Run: Once per long rest, cast one Intrusion you know without spending Surge.

Crow's Lighter

Standard, Relic Save DC 12. No attunement.

A brass lighter that has never been refilled.

  • Flame: It lights in any wind or rain, and under water.
  • Mark: Three times per long rest, as a bonus action, mark a surface or object you touch with a flame only you can see. You know the direction and distance to your marks for one cycle.

More rare relics

Widow Pistol

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A long-barreled pistol with a black grip, worn smooth by one hand.

  • Weapon: Martial ranged weapon. 1d10 piercing damage, range 60/200 feet, reload (6). +1 to attack and damage rolls.
  • Named Round: Three times per long rest, name a creature you can see before you fire. That shot does not need an attack roll. It hits and deals 2d10 piercing damage.

Glass Halo

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A thin band worn at the temples. The world slows a little when it is switched on.

  • Ahead of It: +2 to initiative, and you cannot be surprised.
  • Second Thought: Twice per long rest, take a second reaction in the same round.

Courier's Boots

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

Scuffed boots with a faint hum in the soles. Every courier on Null Row has heard of them.

  • Stride: Your speed increases by 10 feet, and you ignore difficult terrain.
  • Wall Run: Twice per long rest, for 1 minute, you can run along walls and across ceilings as if they were floor.

Chorus Mask

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A half mask with a grille over the mouth. People tend to agree with it.

  • Many Voices: You have advantage on Persuasion and Intimidation checks made while you wear it.
  • Command: Twice per long rest, speak one short instruction to a creature that can hear you. It makes a WIS save. On a failure, it follows the instruction on its next turn if doing so would not directly harm it.

Iron Lung

Rare, Relic Save DC 14. Attunement required.

A chest harness with two sealed canisters. It was built for people who clean the Tether's vents.

  • Sealed: You do not need to breathe, and gas and smoke do not affect you.
  • One More Breath: Once per long rest, when you would drop to 0 hit points, you drop to 1 instead and regain 2d8 hit points at the start of your next turn.

More unique relics

Kingmaker

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A rail rifle with a gold line down the barrel. Three people have held office because of it.

  • Weapon: Martial ranged weapon. 2d10 piercing damage, heavy, two-handed, pierce, range 300/1,200 feet, reload (3). +2 to attack and damage rolls.
  • Through Line: Three charges. Spend one to fire through any wall up to 5 feet thick at a target you know is there. If the shot kills its target, you regain the charge.

The Long Night

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A long armored coat that drinks light.

  • Armor: AC 15 + your DEX modifier (maximum 2). No disadvantage on Stealth, and it uses no Load.
  • Blackout: Once per long rest, as an action, fill a 30-foot radius around you with darkness for 1 minute. You see through it. Nothing else does, including cameras and thermal optics.

Registrar's Seal

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A heavy stamp in a leather case. It was signed out of an office in the Spires and never signed back in.

  • Stamp: Once per cycle, place or lift one Registrar hold, or grant one pass (Shift Pass or Chrome ID), on any file you can hold in your hand. The change is real for one cycle. Then the file goes back to what it was, and someone notices.
  • Authority: A creature that sees the Seal in your hand makes a WIS save. On a failure, it treats you as a Registrar officer for 1 minute.

Heart of Flux

Unique, Relic Save DC 16. Attunement required.

A sealed glass core the size of a fist, warm to the touch, with something slow moving inside.

  • Steady: You are immune to the Glitch table.
  • Capacity: Your Load limit increases by 3.
  • Hunger: At the start of each cycle, the Heart needs one Helix Flux Patch. Without it, the Heart gives you nothing for that cycle.

Legend relics

Paradise Key

Legend, Relic Save DC 18. Attunement required.

A plain steel key on a pink ribbon. It fits a lock that is not there until you need it.

  • Any Door: Once per long rest, the key opens one door, gate, vault, or lock, of any kind, without a check and without setting off an alarm.
  • House Credit: Once per cycle, the key is good for 1,000 chips in a room that is on no map. The house expects them back.

Apex Crown

Legend, Relic Save DC 18. Attunement required (must own a Port Jack).

A thin circlet of dark metal from the counterweight station. It was made for one person, and that person is gone.

  • Crowned: Your Intelligence score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22, and your maximum Surge increases by 3.
  • Decree: Once per long rest, every device within 120 feet answers to you for 1 minute: doors, lifts, cameras, drones, turrets, and vehicles. A creature operating one makes an INT save to keep it.

Relic gear

Relic gear is a part, not a whole object. You fit it to ordinary gear, and that gear becomes something better. An item holds one piece of relic gear. Fitting it takes a Techie one hour, or a short rest for anyone else with the right tools. Relic gear needs no attunement unless its entry says so.

Relics and the story

Every relic has a story that the DM can use. Ask the DM for it at the table. A relic that players do not understand is one the city will try to take.
