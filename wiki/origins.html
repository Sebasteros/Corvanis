Origins

An Origin is where your character was made: the city level, the lab, the clinic, or the road. Each Origin gives ability bonuses (added after you assign your base scores) and one trait.

Origin Bonuses Trait
SubZ Native +2 CON, +1 WIS Undercity Sense
Grid Worker +2 STR, +1 CON Shift Endurance
Spire Heir +2 CHA, +1 INT Old Name
Tether Born +2 DEX, +1 CON Zero-G Stable
Helix Lab Born +2 INT, +1 CON Trial Subject
Vexmar Drifter +2 WIS, +1 DEX Wasteland Trail
Synthetic +2 STR, +1 INT Chassis Build

SubZ Native

You were born on the ground level, where the city's sun is a rumor. You learned to read a room before you learned to read.

  • Undercity Sense: Advantage on Perception checks made to notice someone following you in a crowd or a market. You know the back routes of any SubZ district.
  • You are used to the damp, the noise, and the crowds. You have advantage on saving throws against being deafened.

Grid Worker

You were raised on shift work in the factories and transit lines of the middle city.

  • Shift Endurance: Advantage on saving throws against exhaustion. You can work a full double shift without a penalty.
  • You start with one tool proficiency of your choice (Tinker's kit, Medkit, or Vehicle).

Spire Heir

You grew up above the smog line, where the air is clean and the rules are written for you.

  • Old Name: Advantage on Persuasion checks with Spire residents. Your family name opens doors. It also makes you easy to find.
  • You start with a Spires visitor's pass for one cycle.

Tether Born

You grew up near the counterweight, where gravity is a suggestion. Your bones and reflexes are built for the lift.

  • Zero-G Stable: You take no damage from falls of less than 20 feet. You have advantage on checks to keep your balance on moving surfaces, such as lifts and trains.
  • You may board the Tether with a valid Chrome ID or a sponsor's letter (see The World).

Helix Lab Born

You were grown or treated in a Helix facility. Your body is well-documented, and that is the problem.

  • Trial Subject: You begin with a Load limit 2 higher than your Constitution score. You have a Helix file, and some corporate doors open for you. Some doors close because of it.
  • You start with a Helix record. Helix can call you in for a checkup at any time.

Vexmar Drifter

You crossed the frontier roads south of Vantara, where the advisories never end and the people are hard to count.

  • Wasteland Trail: You can travel twice as far in a day on foot or by road without a travel penalty. You have proficiency in Survival.
  • You start without a Registrar file. Your Civic ID is a temporary one.

Synthetic

You are a manufactured chassis with a mind inside it. Synthetics are common in the city's labor and security work, and they are watched more closely than anyone else.

  • Chassis Build: You do not breathe, eat, or sleep. You are immune to poison and disease. Your Load limit is equal to your Constitution score plus 4.
  • You cannot use Vitalis Cover. Medtech roles can repair you using the Field Workshop rules (see Roles).
  • You have no Registrar standing until the GM assigns you a Civic ID.
