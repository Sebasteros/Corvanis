Surge

Surge is the spark that runs through augmented bodies and rigs. It is the resource that replaces spell slots and most class resources in Corvanis. Surge comes from your nervous system and your augments, not from magic, and it is spent on Intrusions, augment effects, and some Role features.

Your Surge pool

  • Maximum Surge: 2 + the modifier of your Role's Surge ability, with a minimum of 1. Your Role's Surge ability is listed in the Roles table.
  • Buffer and Efficient Surge: Some augments and feats raise your maximum by 1.
  • Regaining Surge: On a long rest, you regain all Surge. On a short rest, you regain half your maximum (rounded down).
  • Spending Surge: Spend Surge as stated in the feature or node. A feature that says "costs 1 Surge" uses your pool. You cannot spend more Surge than you have.

Surge and augments

Augments do not always cost Surge. Where a node or feature says it costs Surge, it uses this pool. Augments that cost Surge are marked in their description.

When Surge runs out

When your Surge pool reaches 0, your augments still work, but any effect that costs Surge fails. You may choose to push past the limit instead:

  • Overdrive: As a bonus action, take 1d6 force damage and gain 1 Surge. Your speed is halved until the end of your next turn. You cannot use Overdrive more than once per short rest.

This keeps netrunners and augmented fighters from running on empty without a clear cost. Overdrive is the only way to get Surge back in the middle of a fight, and it always hurts.

Intrusions and Surge

Netrunner Intrusions use Surge as described in the Netrunner entry. The save DC of an Intrusion is 8 + proficiency bonus + the Netrunner's INT modifier.

Surge in the table

  • A player should always know their Surge number. Write it on the sheet and cross it out when it changes.
  • The GM may award one point of Surge for an excellent plan, a dangerous stunt, or a great moment of roleplay, up to the maximum.
  • Surge is not shared between players. Helping someone else's Surge is done through a feature, not a transfer.
