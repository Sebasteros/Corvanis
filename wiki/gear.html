Services

Vitalis Rescue

Vitalis Rescue is the city's private emergency medicine company. It sells coverage by the cycle. The tiers below are based on what each covered client gets when they are hurt.

Tier Price What it covers
Walk-in 200 cr per treatment Stabilization and basic repairs. No subscription.
Grid Cover 50 cr per cycle Response within the scene in the Grid. Stabilization at 0 hit points, once per cycle. Common augment installs.
Spire Cover 200 cr per cycle Priority response across the Spires. Mil-spec installs at 25 percent off.
Apex Cover 800 cr per cycle Full coverage on the Tether route and in Apex. Prime installs, emergency transport, and any augment repair.

Vitalis logs every treatment to the client's Registrar file. A covered character who is treated gets a note in that file (see Factions). The GM decides how the file is used.
