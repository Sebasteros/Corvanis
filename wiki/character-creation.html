Character Creation

Corvanis characters start at level 2. Build them with the steps below, in order.

Step 1: Origin

Choose one Origin. It gives your ability bonuses and one trait.

Step 2: Role

Choose one Role. It gives your hit die, saving throws, skills, starting gear, and class features for levels 1 and 2.

Step 3: Ability scores

Use the standard array (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8) or point buy (27 points, as in 5e). Assign scores, then add the bonuses from your Origin.

Step 4: Hit points, Proficiency, Load, and Surge

  • Proficiency bonus: +2 at level 2.
  • Hit points: Take the maximum of your hit die at level 1, plus the average of your hit die at level 2, plus your Constitution modifier for each level.
  • Load limit: Equal to your Constitution score. Augments cost Load, and your total cannot go above this limit (see Augments).
  • Surge pool: 2 plus the modifier of your Role's Surge ability, with a minimum of 1 (see Surge).

Step 5: Background

Choose one background below. Each gives two skill proficiencies, a tool or language, and a feature that describes who you know.

Background Skills Feature: Who You Know
Gutter Rat Stealth, Survival Null Row Lookouts. You can always find a bed and a warning in SubZ.
Registered Clerk Investigation, Persuasion Desk Favor. Once per cycle, a Registrar clerk you know can pull one record for you.
Repo Agent Athletics, Intimidation Collection Routes. You know how repo crews move, and can find a hold order's path.
Helix Test Subject Medicine, Insight Trial Records. You have a Helix file, which opens some clinics and some doors.
Dock Hand Athletics, Perception Freight Friends. Lift 7 freight staff give you a second look before they say no.
Street Medic Medicine, Perception Back Room Trust. Unlicensed clinics treat you as one of their own.

Step 6: Access ID and passes

Every character starts with a Civic ID, which opens public areas in every level and lets you use public services.

  • Shift Pass (the Grid): Issued at the Lift 7 desk after a work permit. Some Origins or Backgrounds may start with one.
  • Chrome ID (the Spires and Apex): Only for elite clearance. Almost no starting character has one. The GM decides if your story earns one.

See The World for which levels each pass opens.

Step 7: Credits and debt

  • Starting credits: 1,000 cr.
  • Starting debt: Set by the GM. Suggested starting debt is 5,000 cr, owed to a lender such as Milia Trade Company. Debt grows through fees and interest, and the GM will track it between cycles.

Debt standing

Standing What it means
Clear Your debt is paid or in good standing. No special effects.
Overdue You missed a payment. Collectors may call. You can still move freely.
Flagged You missed several payments. The Registrar may place a hold on your Grid access.
Repossession Notice The lender has issued a notice. The GM decides what this means for your character.

Step 8: Starting augment

Your Role gives you one starting augment, a Tier I node from its line (listed in your Role). Note its Load cost. Augments are explained in Augments and Skill Trees.

Step 9: Gear

Spend your credits on gear from Gear and Services. Your Role lists starting equipment; you may replace it with an equal-value option.

Step 10: Story

Write three lines: who you were before Vantara, what you owe, and who you do not trust. Your GM will build your first session around them.

Rules note: Anything not covered here uses the 5e rule for the same situation.
